TechnologySep 18, 2026

Making technology earn its place

Useful tools should give a little attention back, not just ask for more of it.

Open this note

The best tool in the room is not always the newest one. I keep coming back to a simple test: does this make space for more thought, or does it just give me another place to put it? It's a small question, but a helpful one when everything is promising to change how we work.

Everyday lifeSep 7, 2026

The long way home

A small case for leaving a few minutes unplanned at the end of the day.

Open this note

A route can be efficient and still miss the point. Taking the longer way home has a way of returning the day to me: a new shop, a change in the sky, a thought that had no room to finish itself at the desk. Nothing dramatic happens. That might be why it works.

IdeasAug 24, 2026

Being new at something, on purpose

What changes when the goal is to keep asking, instead of getting good quickly?

Open this note

There is a particular freedom in being a beginner: you have permission to ask the obvious question, to make a clumsy first attempt, to be surprised. Expertise matters. So does remembering what it felt like before things made sense. I want to keep a little of that beginner's mind around.
